About Gamai

Learning that opens
real opportunity

Gamai is an AI-powered learning platform built in Nigeria — connecting quality e-content, trusted tutors and recognised skills so every learner can build the future they want.

Our mission

Bridging learning and opportunity

Too many learners have the drive but not the access — to good content, the right tutor, or a clear path to a career. Gamai brings it together in one place: structured e-content, online and hybrid tutoring, and skills that employers actually want.

From a K-12 student revising for exams to a professional switching careers, we meet learners where they are and walk with them to where they want to be.

Leadership

Leadership Team

Meet the visionary leaders driving Raoatech's mission to transform Nigeria's ICT landscape.

Oludare Olarewaju

CEO & Founder

Oludare Olarewaju is a 3X tech founder with over 15-year experience in management of design and development of enterprise software solutions, impacting sectors like education, taxation, lotteries and gaming, aviation, e-commerce and more. He has managed start-ups with over $1.5M revenues in the last few years.

  • Trained 10,000+ African youths on tech skills in the last 15 years
  • Village Capital accelerator's Future of Work Africa 2022 program participant
  • Design Thinking Coach for University of Lagos undergraduates (2017 & 2018)
  • Amazon Web Service (AWS) Community Builder and speaker
  • President of Association of Nigeria Software Testers with over 1000 members
Education: Graduate of Systems Engineering Department, University of Lagos
Presentations & Publications
Importance of Software Testing (NIPOST) Publication

Temitope Oladele-Olarewaju

Director

Temitope is an ISTQB (International Software Testing Qualifications Board)-certified Quality Assurance Engineer/Software Tester with over 15-year of technical hands-on experience in manual testing, performance testing and management of quality assurance/software testing teams for different organisations.

Her experience cuts across sectors such as: telecoms industry (MTN, 9Mobile and others), banking sector (First Bank and others), edtech industry (Raoatech and others), Oil and Gas industry, gaming and lotteries.

Education: B.Sc Hons in Computer Science from Olabisi Onabanjo University, Nigeria

Our full team consists of over 50 talented professionals dedicated to delivering excellence in ICT solutions and training.
